Welcome Message⌄

Welcome to Applied UX Topics and Guest Lectures. Across six weeks you'll hear from practising UX professionals and work through the full Discover–Define–Develop–Deliver process toward your UX Design Award portfolio. Come with questions for our guest speakers!

Learning Outcomes⌄

On completion, students will be able to: (1) critically apply UX research and design methods to a real-world brief; (2) evaluate design decisions against user needs and business constraints; (3) communicate design rationale clearly to non-specialist stakeholders.

Accessibility in Learn.gold⌄

All slides are available in advance with alt text on images. Recap videos include captions. Contact Disability Services or the module leader directly if you need materials in an alternative format.
